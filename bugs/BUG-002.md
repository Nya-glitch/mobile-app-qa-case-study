# BUG-002: Typo in splash screen tagline

- Severity: Low
- Phase / Feature: Phase 1 / Test 1.2 Cold launch (splash screen)
- Device & Android version: Redmi A5, Android 15
- App version: 0.1.2
- Reproducible: Always

## Steps to reproduce
1. Close the app completely from Recents.
2. Tap the Begah Homes icon.
3. Read the tagline under the logo on the splash screen.

## Expected result
The tagline is spelled correctly: "simplifying real estate".

## Actual result
The tagline reads "simplifiying real estate" (extra "i").

## Evidence
screenshots/BUG-002-typo-tagline.jpg

## Notes
Cosmetic text error. The tagline is part of the logo image, which is being replaced, so this may be fixed by the new logo. Logged so there is a record of what v0.1.2 shipped with.
