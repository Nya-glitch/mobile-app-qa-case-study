# BUG-005: App stays on "Cannot reach the server" after the connection returns

- Severity: High
- Phase / Feature: Phase 1 / Test 1.5 Launch with no internet (Portfolio screen)
- Device & Android version: Redmi A5, Android 15
- App version: 0.1.2
- Reproducible: Always

## Steps to reproduce
1. Log in and close the app fully from Recents.
2. Turn on Airplane mode.
3. Open the app. The Portfolio screen shows "Cannot reach the server."
4. Turn Airplane mode off and wait about 10 seconds.
5. Try to recover: pull down to refresh, then tap Approvals and back to Portfolio.

## Expected result
Once the connection is back, the Portfolio loads automatically or when the user refreshes.

## Actual result
The error message stays on screen. Refreshing and switching tabs do not clear it. The only fix is to close the app fully from Recents and open it again.
