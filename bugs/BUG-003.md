# BUG-003: Splash screen logo is cut off at the edges

- Severity: Low
- Phase / Feature: Phase 1 / Test 1.2 Cold launch (splash screen)
- Device & Android version: Redmi A5, Android 15
- App version: 0.1.2
- Reproducible: Always

## Steps to reproduce
1. Close the app completely from Recents.
2. Tap the Begah Homes icon.
3. Look at the logo on the splash screen.

## Expected result
The full logo is visible, with space around it.

## Actual result
The logo is clipped on both sides. The first letter "B" is cut off on the left and the last letter "S" is cut off on the right.

## Evidence
screenshots/BUG-003-logo-cut-off.jpg

## Notes
Cosmetic only; the app launches normally. The logo image is being replaced, so this may be fixed by the new logo. Logged so there is a record of what v0.1.2 shipped with. Worth re-checking on other screen sizes.
