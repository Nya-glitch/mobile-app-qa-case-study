# BUG-001: App icon tagline is unreadable

- Severity: Low
- Phase / Feature: Phase 1 / Test 1.1 App icon
- Device & Android version: Redmi A5, Android 15
- App version: 0.1.2
- Reproducible: Always

## Steps to reproduce
1. Install Begah Homes v0.1.2.
2. Open the app drawer.
3. Look at the Begah Homes icon.

## Expected result
All text in the icon, including the tagline under the logo, is readable at normal size.

## Actual result
The "Begah Homes" logo text is readable, but the small tagline below it is too small and low-contrast to read.

## Evidence
screenshots/P1-1.1-icon.jpg

## Notes
Cosmetic only; the app works normally. Suggested fix: remove the tagline from the icon, or enlarge it and increase the contrast.
