# BUG-004: "Tap below to retry" shown but there is no retry button

- Severity: Medium
- Phase / Feature: Phase 1 / Test 1.5 Launch with no internet (Portfolio screen)
- Device & Android version: Redmi A5, Android 15
- App version: 0.1.2
- Reproducible: Always

## Steps to reproduce
1. Log in and close the app fully from Recents.
2. Turn on Airplane mode.
3. Open the app.
4. Look at the Portfolio screen.

## Expected result
The error message is followed by a visible Retry button, as the text says.

## Actual result
The screen shows "Cannot reach the server. Check your connection. Tap below to retry." but there is no button below the message.

## Evidence
screenshots/BUG-004-no-retry-button.jpg

## Notes
The offline message itself is clear and the app does not crash. The instruction is misleading because there is nothing to tap.
